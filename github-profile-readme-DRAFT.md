# WayMakr NYC

WayMakr NYC — free crisis help in New York City. Food, shelter, safety, rent — your next step forward. Every listing is checked against its official source.

WayMakr helps New Yorkers find real help in plain language — food, shelter, eviction and rent help, safety at home, mental health support, benefits, legal help, and more. Every listing is shown with its source and the date it was last checked, so the help you find is help you can trust.

**Visit:** [waymakrnyc.org](https://waymakrnyc.org)

Here, no one walks alone.
